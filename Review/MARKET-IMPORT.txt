Out Of Service - market snapshot, 2026-11-22

Open the repository root as a Unity project using Unity 6000.6.8f1.
The team project and the market source both use URP 17.6.0.
After Unity finishes importing, open Assets/Scenes/MarketPrototype_v7.unity
and enter Play mode. The intended game output is 1920 x 1080.

Layout: B1 mall and convenience shops; 1F groceries, homewares and appliances;
2F fashion and customer services. Both emergency stairs and escalators connect
the levels. This is a work-in-progress composite Korean supermarket, not a
replica of a specific store. Product and shop brands are original parodies.

The team's existing scenes, characters, Packages and ProjectSettings are kept.
The existing build scene list is unchanged. Three shared Assets/Settings files
carry the market's renderer/pipeline serialization and ambient-occlusion setup;
review those changes against other team scenes before merging this branch.

Standalone Windows build from the saved market scene:
  Unity.exe -batchmode -quit -projectPath <checkout> -executeMethod MarketPlayerBuildReview.BuildPlayer -logFile <build-log>
Output: Builds/MarketReview/Market.exe and build-result.txt (not committed).
Use a normal graphics device; reflection capture and scene regeneration need it.

Regeneration (optional, replaces the generated market scene):
  Unity menu: Out Of Service > Build Market Prototype V7
  Or executeMethod MarketPlayerBuildReview.RebuildSceneAndPlayer to regenerate
  the scene and build the Windows player together. Save other scene work first.
  Keep gameplay and art changes in the generator sources if they must survive
  regeneration. Tools contains the original packaging/sign artwork generators.
