# Package artwork sources

`catalog_photo_atlas_01.png` and `catalog_photo_atlas_02.png` were generated for this project with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. They show isolated, unbranded grocery and household product photographs on a 4 x 4 grid.

`pack_00_0.png` through `pack_29_5.png` (180 fronts across 30 categories) were generated locally by `Tools/GenerateMarketPacks.ps1`, which places crops from those atlases inside original Korean package layouts. They use familiar Korean retail product and brand names as visual references for this class prototype. They are not official package artwork or scans. Replace or license brand names and exact visual identity before public distribution.

Product assortment references: [Nongshim](https://www.nongshim.com/main/index) and [CJ Hetbahn](https://m.cj.co.kr/kr/brands/hetbahn). In-store shelving references: [Arneg Korea Homeplus Gayang installation](https://www.arneg.co.kr/en/installations/homeplus).

## Korean category label redesign — 2026-11-10
42 fronts (water, soda, sports drinks, tea drinks, canned coffee, laundry care and paper goods) are original procedural artwork generated by Tools/DrawRetailLabels.ps1 through Tools/GenerateMarketPacks.ps1. Product names identify the referenced goods; these are approximations, not official scans or exact brand artwork. No purchased assets or downloaded pack photos are included.
Visual/category references: https://id.jpdc.kr/samdasoo/products.htm ; https://www.donga-otsuka.co.kr/ ; https://donga-bluemall.com/category/포카리스웨트/32
Original geometry: MarketSpecialtyRetailV7.cs (paper rolls, dispenser tissue, pump bottles, handled laundry jugs, rectangular paste tubs).
Existing Poly Haven scan normal maps are enabled and their existing CC0 provenance remains in Art/Models/SOURCES.md. Derived scan LOD meshes retain that provenance.
LOD API reference: https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.7/script-reference/unityengine/lodgroup

Tools/GeneratePriceTickets.ps1 produces original Korean shelf tickets. Prices and barcodes are fictional set dressing, not current retail prices or valid inventory codes. Bag UVs and carton face UVs are oriented independently on both sides. Printed sign front and back surfaces are separate and backface culled.
